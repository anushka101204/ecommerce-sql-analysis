# Business Insights

## 1. Revenue Concentration
Top 10 products account for ~40% of total revenue — indicates heavy
dependence on hero products. Recommend diversifying promotional focus.

## 2. Customer Segmentation (RFM)
- **Champions** (~8% of customers) generate ~35% of revenue
- **Loyal** (~20%) generate ~30%
- **At Risk + Lost** (~35%) — reactivation campaigns recommended

## 3. Category Performance
- Electronics has highest AOV (~3x Fashion)
- Books have high review frequency but low revenue per unit
- Home category is underperforming — potential growth area

## 4. Dormant Customers
~12% of registered customers never placed an order — onboarding funnel
needs improvement.

## 5. Product Affinity
High correlation between Electronics + Accessories purchases —
bundle promotions recommended.

## 6. Refund/Cancellation
~7% order cancellation rate — investigate payment failures (COD ~40% fail rate).
